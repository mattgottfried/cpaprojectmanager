Build 14 - what's new (please test):

- GOOGLE DRIVE IS NOW WHERE DOCUMENTS LIVE. First: Settings > Google > Reconnect to Google and approve Drive access.
- Pick a client's (or job's) Drive folder, then add a scan, photo, file, letter PDF, quote PDF or invoice PDF: it is filed into that folder and the app keeps a link. No folder yet? It stays in the app with a note.
- "Move to Google Drive" (long-press a document, or Add Document > Move in-app files) sends existing files to Drive.
- Settings > Google > "File new documents in Drive" turns it off.

Check: file opens from the link; the file shows in Drive; nothing in Drive was changed or deleted.
